TITLE: Firefly Meadow - Dusk
PROMPT: FIREFLY MEADOW - dusk meadow: grass silhouettes bottom 20% #1a2a1e, sky #2a3a5e->#0d1428. 60 fireflies: glow points (#d4ff6f, two-pass draw: soft halo + bright core) each wandering with a blink envelope (two fast pulses then 1.5s off, random phase). Pointer = lantern: fireflies within 140px flee (steer away) and their blink rate doubles; CALM button turns the lantern off and they drift home. WIND slider adds drift bias. One white 'moon-moth' on a figure-8 path. Caption 'FIREFLIES / 60 - DUSK'. Enchanting, quiet, alive.
DESCRIPTION: A quiet dusk scene: vertical sky gradient from #2a3a5e to #0d1428 with a few twinkling stars and a soft cratered moon, 60 fireflies wandering on damped random-walk motion, each with a two-pulse-then-off blink envelope and random phase, drawn in two passes (soft wide #d4ff6f halo plus a bright near-white core). The pointer acts as a warm lantern: fireflies inside 140px steer away with doubled blink rate and a radial glow marks the lantern. A swaying field of #1a2a1e grass silhouettes with seed heads fills the bottom 20%, and a single white moon-moth traces a lemniscate figure-8 with flapping wings and a faint motion streak.
TECHNIQUES: two-pass glow rendering (halo + core) on a DPR-aware canvas, Gaussian double-pulse blink envelopes with per-fly phase offsets, damped random-walk wander with home-anchoring, radius-based pointer repulsion steering, lemniscate parametric moth path with sine wing-flutter, wind-driven grass sway via quadratic curves, layered sky gradient with twinkling star field and vignette.
INTERACTION: pointer over the canvas becomes a lantern that scares and quickens nearby fireflies; the CALM button toggles the lantern off so fireflies drift back home; the WIND slider (0-100) adds a horizontal drift bias to the flies and the grass sway.
