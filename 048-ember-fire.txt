TITLE: EMBER FIRE
PROMPT: EMBER FIRE - cellular-automaton fire, full-bleed. Cell grid (4px), classic fire palette: #0a0505, #4a0f0a, #a02812, #e05a1f, #ffb43a, #ffe9b0; probabilities + WIND slider (0-1) tilting spread; the bottom row re-ignites continuously (log line); SPARK mode: drag ignites a line of cells. Hot cells occasionally eject rising fading ember dots (max 200). Performance: typed-array sim updated in chunks; render via ImageData at grid resolution scaled up with image-smoothing off (crisp cells). Caption 'EMBER - GRID 120x70'. Mesmeric, warm, dangerous.
DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed cellular-automaton fire on a 120x70 grid of 6-level heat cells, driven by the classic flame palette from black-brown through crimson and orange to pale yellow. Each frame runs two typed-array simulation substeps: heat decays with probabilistic hold/flicker, and cells spread to the row above plus wind-tilted left/right neighbours (the WIND slider 0-1 reweights lateral probability). The bottom log row re-ignites continuously so the fire never dies. Rendering writes palette lookups into an ImageData buffer at native grid resolution, then scales up with image-smoothing off for crisp blocky cells; up to 200 ember dots ejected by the hottest cells drift upward with additive glow. In SPARK mode, dragging a pointer across the canvas ignites a short line of cells, letting the viewer carve new flame paths through the automaton.
TECHNIQUES: typed-array (Uint8Array) cellular automaton with chunked row updates, probabilistic heat decay and upward/wind-biased spread, continuous bottom-row re-ignition (log line), palette-lookup ImageData rendering at grid resolution, pixelated upscale with image-smoothing disabled, additive-blend rising ember particles with soft glow halos, pointer-drag ignition, DPR-aware full-bleed canvas, dt-free rAF loop with try/guard
INTERACTION: WIND slider (0.00-1.00) tilts the fire's lateral spread from left to right in real time; SPARK toggle button enables dragging across the canvas to ignite a line of cells under the pointer; otherwise the fire simulates continuously and full-bleed
