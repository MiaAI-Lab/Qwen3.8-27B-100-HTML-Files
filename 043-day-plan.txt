TITLE: A DAY, BLOCKED
PROMPT: A DAY, BLOCKED - minimal time-block planner. BG #f6f4ee, ink #20211f, accent #3a6cff. Vertical timeline 06:00->23:00: left rail with mono times + hour hairlines; 7 colored rounded blocks (WAKE, DEEP WORK, LUNCH, GYM, REVIEW, CALL, SLEEP) with soft shadow; the red 'now' line #d63a3a with a dot sits at the real clock position and creeps; past blocks dim to 60%. Click a block: it expands showing 3 sub-bullets + a DONE toggle (satisfying strikethrough + tiny spark). '+ NEW BLOCK' inserts an editable contenteditable 30-min block. Keys up/down move focus. Calm, useful, pretty.
DESCRIPTION: A calm paper-toned planner that turns the 06:00-23:00 span into a vertical timeline: a mono left rail of hour labels with hairline rules, and seven soft-shadowed color blocks stacked at their real positions. A red "now" line with a pulsing dot tracks the live clock each second, dimming any block whose end time has passed to 60% opacity. Clicking a block slides open a white detail card beneath it with three sub-bullets and a DONE pill; toggling done draws a strikethrough across the block name and fires a tiny six-particle spark. A fixed pill dock at the bottom holds a key hint and a '+ NEW BLOCK' button that finds the first open 30-minute gap, inserts a dashed 'fresh' block, and makes its title a contenteditable field (blur/Enter commits the rename). Arrow up/down walk keyboard focus through the blocks in timeline order.
TECHNIQUES: absolute-positioned timeline with percentage math from real minute values, live-clock 'now' line with pulsing dot and out-of-range dimming, per-block detail cards opened by toggle class, strikethrough via ::after width transition, WAAPI particle spark with setTimeout fallback, first-fit gap search for new blocks, contenteditable inline rename, keyboard focus ring via layered box-shadows, responsive clamp() type down to 360px
INTERACTION: click a block to expand/collapse its detail card; DONE button toggles completion with spark; '+ NEW BLOCK' button inserts an editable 30-minute block into the first free gap (title editable on blur/Enter); ArrowUp/ArrowDown move focus through blocks in order; live HUD clock and red now-line update every second automatically
