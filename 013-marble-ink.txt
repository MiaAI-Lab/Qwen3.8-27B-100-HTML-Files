TITLE: Marble Ink
PROMPT: MARBLE INK - scroll paper-marbling. Long scroll page (#f5f0e6 paper) with a fixed full-viewport canvas behind: curl-noise flow field drives thousands of thin ink strokes (3 inks: #1e2a4a, #c0392b, #e8b13f) that marble across the sheet; as the user scrolls, the field's angle rotates (scrollY -> rotation + shear) so each of the 4 text sections reveals differently-marbled patterns. Four translucent paper cards hold short poetic paragraphs. Right edge: scroll progress bar. Deterministic seed. Strokes drawn once into a persistent canvas - only new strokes added as scroll progresses.
DESCRIPTION: A long four-section scroll on warm paper (#f5f0e6) with a fixed full-viewport canvas behind. A curl-noise flow field (deterministic mulberry32 seed, 48x48 value-noise grid with bilinear smoothing) drives thousands of ultra-thin semi-transparent ink strokes in indigo #1e2a4a, red #c0392b and gold #e8b13f. Each stroke walks 16-28 steps along the field, and the field's angle rotates with scrollY (plus a sinusoidal shear term and a slow lateral offset), so scrolling into each of the four sections reveals a differently-marbled region of the same canvas. Strokes are drawn once and never redrawn — only new ones are appended as scroll progresses, up to a 6400 cap. Translucent paper cards hold four short poetic paragraphs with ghost roman numerals, a gradient scroll-progress track on the right edge, and a monospace tracked-caps HUD caption.
TECHNIQUES: curl-noise flow field, deterministic seeded value noise, persistent additive canvas strokes, scroll-driven field rotation + shear, translucent card layering, gradient progress bar, paper grain background
INTERACTION: scroll only — scrolling down appends new marble strokes and rotates the flow field; right-edge progress bar tracks scroll; no buttons or input keys
