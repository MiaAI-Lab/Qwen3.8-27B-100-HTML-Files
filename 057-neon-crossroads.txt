TITLE: Neon Crossroads
PROMPT: NEON CROSSROADS - one-point-perspective night street (canvas): road with dashed lane lines to the vanishing point; building facades both sides with random lit windows (warm #ffd27a / cool #7fd4ff); three neon signs 'BAR', 'RAMEN', '24H' in glowing stroked text, one flickers; rain streak particles + wet-road reflection (mirrored blurred copy of the lower scene, alpha .15); car headlight pairs pass at intervals. Pointer pans the crossroads slightly (parallax). RAIN chip toggles precipitation; NIGHT / DUSK chips. Caption 'CROSSROADS - 11:47 PM' with a live clock.
DESCRIPTION: A neon-soaked one-point-perspective night street rendered entirely on canvas: a dark road with glowing dashed lanes converging to the vanishing point, building facades on both sides seeded with randomly lit warm amber and cool cyan windows, and three stroked neon signs (BAR, RAMEN, 24H) with the 24H sign flickering. Rain streak particles fall diagonally while a mirrored, blurred 0.15-alpha copy of the upper scene paints the wet road's reflection. Car headlight pairs (with red taillights) travel toward and away from the vanishing point at intervals, and a real-time clock keeps the caption alive.
TECHNIQUES: one-point perspective projection, neon glow via layered shadowBlur stroked text, flicker modulation, mirrored offscreen-canvas wet-road reflection with blur filter, rain particle streaks, parallax pointer panning, seeded random window grids, radial-gradient headlight pools
INTERACTION: pointer pans the crossroads with smoothed parallax; RAIN chip toggles precipitation on/off; NIGHT / DUSK chips switch between the deep-night and dusky purple palettes; caption shows a live clock time
