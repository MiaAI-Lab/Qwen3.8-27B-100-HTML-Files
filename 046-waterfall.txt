DESCRIPTION: A moody gorge rendered on a single DPR-aware canvas. The cliff face is a cached background layer: dark rock #2a2e35 with ragged silhouette edges, drawn strata lines, distant conifer silhouettes, and a flat lip ledge over a deep pool #1a3a4a. The falls themselves are a pool of up to 600 particle streams spawned at the lip with x-jitter and depth-scaled alpha, accelerated by gravity; each impact spawns expanding elliptical splash rings and soft rising spray-mist dots, with a broad additive mist veil at the base. Faint god-ray refractions angle down through the sheet, and horizontal shimmer lines drift across the pool. Foreground boulder silhouettes and two swaying hanging vines frame the scene. A FLOW slider scales particle count, speed, splash size and mist density; a SOUND toggle builds brown noise through a low-pass filter on user gesture.
TECHNIQUES: cached offscreen background/foreground canvas layers, particle system with depth-based alpha, gravity integration, elliptical expanding splash rings, additive-blend mist and god-ray gradients, bezier-drawn swaying vines, strata line work, DPR-aware resizing, WebAudio brown noise through low-pass filter, dt-clamped rAF loop with try/guard
INTERACTION: FLOW range slider (weak to raging) continuously rescales fall intensity; SOUND button toggles filtered brown-noise ambience (created on user gesture, gain/filter follow flow); responsive canvas resizes the whole scene
