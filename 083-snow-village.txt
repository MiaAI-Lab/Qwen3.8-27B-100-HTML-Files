TITLE: Snow Village Iso
PROMPT: SNOW VILLAGE ISO - isometric 6x6 tile village (canvas, 2:1 projection): some tiles hold tiny houses (3-face prisms with warm windows), pine trees (stacked triangles, snow-capped). Continuous snowfall: 250 flakes, 3 depth layers, drift from the WIND slider. Accumulation: every 30s real time adds 1px snow cap to roofs + tiles (max 6px) - the village slowly buries. Click a tile: shove snow (cap drops 3px, puff particles). LANTERNS toggle: windows + 4 streetlamps glow warm vs cold night. Moon + stars above. Caption 'VILLAGE / SNOWFALL - DEPTH 3 PX'.
DESCRIPTION: A full-viewport isometric winter diorama rendered per frame on canvas in 2:1 projection over a 6x6 tile field. The village alternates grass-green checkerboard tiles with hand-drawn houses (two shaded wall faces, a split gable roof, perspective-transformed windows) and three-tier snow-capped pines, arranged back-to-front in painter's order. 250 snowflakes fall in three depth layers with size/alpha/speed separation and sinusoidal sway; the WIND slider biases their horizontal drift. A slow accumulation loop adds 1px of snow cap to every roof and tile every 30 seconds of real time up to 6px, so the village visibly buries while the HUD caption tracks average depth. Clicking a tile shoves its cap down 3px and fires a burst of white puff particles. The LANTERNS toggle flips between a cold blue dusk (windows dim, lamp globes gray) and a warm night (amber windows, glowing streetlamps with radial halos, deeper sky, brighter star twinkle). A cratered moon and 40 twinkling stars sit in the sky.
TECHNIQUES: isometric 2:1 tile projection, painter's-algorithm depth ordering, canvas gradient skies, perspective-transformed window quads, layered parallax snowfall, particle puffs, radial glow halos, time-based accumulation state, DPR-aware resize
INTERACTION: WIND slider drifts snowfall left/right; LANTERNS toggle switches windows and 4 streetlamps between warm glow and cold night; clicking/tapping any tile shoves its snow cap down 3px with a particle puff; the caption reports live average snow depth.
