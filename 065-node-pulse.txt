TITLE: Node Pulse
PROMPT: NODE PULSE - signal editor toy. Dark #0e1116 with faint grid. Six glass node cards (120x80: title + left in-port #27d3f2 dot + right out-port #ff2e88 dot), draggable by pointer. CONNECT mode: click an out-port then an in-port to link; wires = bezier curves auto-routed between ports, redrawn live. Every 1.2s the source nodes emit bright dots that travel parametrically along wires and split across branches, lighting the target port on arrival. RUN / PAUSE, SHUFFLE (new random topology, same nodes), CLEAR WIRES. Caption 'SIGNAL / NET - 6 NODES, LIVE' + pulse counter.
DESCRIPTION: A dark signal-desk with a faint 44px engineering grid over a radial ambient wash. Six frosted-glass node cards (OSC, MIX, GATE, VCO, ENV, OUT) each carry a cyan in-port and a magenta out-port; cards are grabbed and repositioned with the pointer. In CONNECT mode a clicked out-port glows, a dashed ghost line trails the cursor, and clicking an in-port drops a cubic-bezier wire that is re-routed live as nodes move. Every 1.2s the topology's source nodes fire warm incandescent dots that travel parametrically down each bezier, split across outgoing branches (up to three hops), and flash the destination port cyan on arrival. A tabular pulse counter tallies every emission.
TECHNIQUES: glassmorphism node cards on dark grid, cubic-bezier wire routing with control-point mirroring, parametric point-along-curve pulse animation, branch-splitting pulse propagation, port glow flash decay, dashed ghost link line, radial ambient gradient, tracked-caps HUD
INTERACTION: drag node cards with pointer; CONNECT toggle then click out-port then in-port to link; RUN/PAUSE halts emission; SHUFFLE regenerates a random DAG topology; CLEAR WIRES removes all links; pulse counter in footer
