TITLE: The Glass Parlor
PROMPT: GLASS PARLOR - ice cream menu as 3D glass. Soft pink #f7e8ec bg with faint polka dots. 'THE GLASS PARLOR' rounded-caps title with a cherry accent. Four glass cups (CSS: rounded cups, rgba(255,255,255,.12), highlight streaks), each holding two scoops (perfect circles, two-tone shading) - VANILLA #f4efe4, STRAWBERRY #f2a4b0, MINT #a8e0c0, CHOCOLATE #8a5a3a; each scoop has one JS-animated drip slowly sliding down the cup wall in a loop. Hover a cup: it lifts + scoops wobble; click: drip burst + flavor name chip pops. SUNDAE button adds a cherry + chocolate drizzle drawn progressively (zigzag). Caption 'EST. 1962 - 4 FLAVORS'.
DESCRIPTION: A vintage ice-cream parlor menu rendered as four translucent glass cups standing on a soft polka-dot pink field. Each cup is a rounded CSS vessel with a 12% white glass fill, inset glow, two vertical highlight streaks, and two two-tone scoops in the brief's palette. A JS rAF loop continuously slides drips down each cup wall on an eased, non-uniform schedule so the cups never look synchronized. Hovering lifts a cup on a springy cubic-bezier and wobbles both scoops; clicking fires a burst of falling flavor-colored droplets and pops a pill-shaped flavor chip above the glass. The SUNDAE toggle drops a glossy cherry onto every cup and progressively draws a chocolate zigzag drizzle via stroke-dashoffset animation. Tracked-caps footer reads EST. 1962 - 4 FLAVORS.
TECHNIQUES: CSS polka-dot background, layered glass cup (rgba fill + inset shadows + highlight streaks), two-tone radial scoop circles, JS rAF eased drip animation, hover lift with spring easing, wobble keyframes, click particle burst, SVG pathLength stroke-dashoffset progressive drizzle, pop-in chip keyframes, system font tracked-caps HUD
INTERACTION: Hover a cup to lift it and wobble the scoops; click or press Enter/Space on a cup for a drip burst and flavor chip pop; toggle the SUNDAE button to add cherry and chocolate drizzle to all cups.
