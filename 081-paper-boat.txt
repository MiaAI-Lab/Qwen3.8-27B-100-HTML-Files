TITLE: Paper Boat on Waves
PROMPT: PAPER BOAT ON WAVES - serene sea. Sky #dfeaf2; sea = three layered sine wave bands (canvas, #4a7a9e / #3a6a8e / #2a5a7e, different parallax speeds). A white origami boat (SVG, #f4f4f2 + fold shadow) rides the main wave: y = wave(x,t), pitch = wave slope - it bobs and tilts like it's real. WIND slider: amplitude + frequency (calm -> choppy). Click the sea: a ripple ring at the click + a traveling wave impulse nudges the boat. Occasional tiny duck crosses. SUNNY / STORMY chip: storm = #5a6a7a sky, rain streaks, whitecap foam dots. Caption 'BOAT / SEA - WIND 3 KN'.
DESCRIPTION: A serene full-viewport seascape built on a single DPR-aware canvas. The sky is a soft vertical wash from #dfeaf2 to near-white with a low sun halo; the sea is three stacked sine bands (#4a7a9e, #3a6a8e, #2a5a7e) with distinct parallax speeds and secondary harmonics so the surface reads as depth. A hand-built SVG origami boat in #f4f4f2 with fold crease lines and a drop shadow rides the front band: its y follows wave(x,t) and its rotation follows the local wave slope, so it bobs and tilts like a real paper boat. A wind slider (0-10 KN) drives both amplitude and frequency from glassy calm to choppy surf, and the live caption reports the current wind. Clicking the water spawns an elliptical ripple ring and a traveling wave impulse that visibly nudges the boat sideways. A tiny duck drifts across every so often, and a Sunny/Stormy chip cross-fades the scene into a #5a6a7a storm sky with cloud masses, slanted rain streaks, and whitecap foam dots on breaking crests.
TECHNIQUES: layered canvas sine-wave parallax, runtime SVG geometry, canvas pixel-ratio scaling, eased state crossfade (sunny/storm), traveling impulse waves, procedural whitecaps, rain streak system, duck crossing loop, CSS clamp() typography, pill HUD controls
INTERACTION: WIND slider adjusts wave amplitude and frequency; clicking the sea spawns ripple rings and a traveling impulse that nudges the boat; SUNNY/STORMY chip toggles storm sky, rain, clouds and whitecaps; caption updates wind value live
