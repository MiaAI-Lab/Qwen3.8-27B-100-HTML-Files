TITLE: Starfield / Warp-9
PROMPT: STARFIELD WARP - artful classic warp. 900 stars projected in 3D (z-depth -> screen), 3 color tiers by depth: white, warm #ffd9a0, cool #7fa8ff. WARP slider 0->1: 0 = slow drift, 1 = streaks (draw line from previous to current position, length proportional to speed). Pointer eases the vanishing focus point toward the cursor. 'EVENT HORIZON' button: white flash pulse, all stars streak inward then respawn outside. Static subtle nebula (two large radial gradients) behind. HUD: 'VELOCITY 0.42C' live. Caption 'STARFIELD / WARP-9'.
DESCRIPTION: A full-viewport canvas renders a classic 3D starfield warp: 900 stars live in a radial coordinate space and are projected by 1/z scaling onto the screen each frame. A two-part radial-gradient nebula in violet and deep magenta sits static behind the star layer, with a soft vignette pulling the eye to the vanishing point. Color tiering (white, warm amber, cool blue) is assigned by depth so distant stars read as the warm/cool fringe and near stars burn white. The WARP slider bends the starfield from a gentle drift at 0 into long motion streaks at 1, where each star is stroked from its previous to its current projected position with length proportional to speed. A live monospace HUD reports the computed VELOCITY in C-fractions and the current mode (DRIFT, STREAK, EVENT HORIZON).
TECHNIQUES: 3D z-projection (1/z scaling), depth-tiered star palette, motion streak lines via previous-frame projection, eased vanishing-point tracking, dual static radial-gradient nebula, vignette falloff, white flash pulse on event horizon, DPR-aware canvas resizing, live HUD readouts
INTERACTION: WARP slider (0-1) blends drift into streaks; pointer movement eases the vanishing focus point toward the cursor and back to center on release; EVENT HORIZON button fires a white flash pulse while all stars surge inward and respawn far outside; HUD velocity readout updates live
