TITLE: Sumi Brush
PROMPT: SUMI BRUSH - ink calligraphy toy. Washi paper #f2ede2 with subtle fiber texture (tiny noise dots). Brush = pointer: strokes drawn as many overlapping circles whose radius varies with speed (slow = wide, fast = thin) and ink density with hold duration; low 'pressure' ends in a dry-brush sparse dot spray. Colors: sumi black #1a1917; SEAL tool stamps a square red seal #c23a2e with a glyph. WET toggle: strokes slowly bloom outward (radial alpha) like bleed. FOLD button: a paper corner folds up in CSS 3D revealing the back. CLEAR wipes. Caption 'SUMI - PRACTICE 03'. Zen, analog feel.
DESCRIPTION: A full-viewport washi sheet under a dark workshop chrome. Pointer strokes are deposited as chains of overlapping sumi-black circles: radius shrinks with pointer speed (slow presses lay a wide, calm line; fast flicks go thin), alpha deepens the longer the stroke is held, and every release finishes with a sparse dry-brush dot spray at the tail. Each stroke is baked to an offscreen canvas so the live layer only animates active ones. The WET toggle lets fresh strokes bloom radially for about three seconds before settling. The SEAL tool stamps a slightly rotated square red seal with a carved 印 glyph and rough ink edges. FOLD raises a CSS-3D paper corner with a shadowed back face; CLEAR wipes the sheet.
TECHNIQUES: pointer-speed modulated dot chains, hold-time ink density, dry-brush spray on release, radial alpha bloom (wet-ink simulation), offscreen bake layer, washi fiber noise + long-fiber streaks, vignette, CSS 3D corner fold with drop-shadow, tracked-caps HUD
INTERACTION: pointer draws strokes anywhere on the paper; BRUSH/SEAL toggle tools; WET toggles bloom; FOLD raises the corner; CLEAR wipes; hover highlights on all controls
