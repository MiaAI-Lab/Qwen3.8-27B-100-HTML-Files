TITLE: Reaction / Bloom - F.K Map
PROMPT: REACTION BLOOM - Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion, full-bleed canvas (256x144 grid, two typed-array buffers). Colors: bg #0a0d14, substance bands #1d3a52 -> mid #7a5cff -> living #46ff9e. Default F=0.054, K=0.062; live sliders F (0.03-0.06) and K (0.04-0.08) morph the regime (cells -> coral -> rings -> chaos). INOCULATE drops a seed blob at center; CLEAR resets to uniform. Render via ImageData at grid res, upscaled with smoothing ON (soft organic blobs - never crisp). Step sim in chunks across frames to stay smooth. Caption 'REACTION / BLOOM - F.K MAP' + regime name.
DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion simulation on a 256x144 grid using two Float32Array state buffers, stepping the inhibitor/activator system in per-frame chunks so the animation stays smooth even on modest hardware. The activator field is mapped through a 256-entry color LUT that runs from the deep background #0a0d14 through steel-blue #1d3a52, into violet #7a5cff, and up to a living green #46ff9e, giving the patterns their organic bioluminescent character. The field is written into an ImageData at native grid resolution, blitted to a small offscreen canvas, then upscaled to the viewport with image smoothing ON so every blob stays soft and never crisp. Two live sliders drive F (0.03-0.06) and K (0.04-0.08, default 0.054/0.062), morphing the regime from cellular spots through coral growth to traveling rings and full chaos; a regime badge in the footer names the current territory. INOCULATE drops a seed blob at the center (plus a few stray specks) and CLEAR resets the field to uniform. A soft vignette seats the simulation against the dark page, and the caption reads 'REACTION / BLOOM - F.K MAP' in tracked caps.
TECHNIQUES: Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion PDE on a 256x144 grid, two typed-array double-buffer swap, Laplacian finite-difference stencil, feed/kill parameter regimes, 256-entry color LUT banding (#1d3a52 -> #7a5cff -> #46ff9e), ImageData at grid resolution, offscreen canvas upscale with smoothing ON for soft blobs, chunked per-frame stepping with time budget, radial-gradient vignette, tracked-caps HUD and caption
INTERACTION: F and K sliders morph the simulation regime live (cells -> coral -> rings -> chaos); INOCULATE button drops a seed blob at the center; CLEAR button resets the field to uniform; regime badge in the footer names the current F.K territory
