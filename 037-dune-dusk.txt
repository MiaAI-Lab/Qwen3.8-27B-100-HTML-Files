DESCRIPTION: A still, painterly desert at dusk rendered entirely in canvas. A low golden sun with a wide radial glow hangs over four smooth bezier dune ridges in warm terracotta to deep plum; each ridge is shaded with a down-slope vignette so the far side falls into long shadow. A saguaro silhouette and two rock shapes sit on the nearest crest. Three tiny v-birds drift slowly across the upper sky. A thin band near the horizon is re-blended every frame with a sine displacement to read as heat shimmer without disturbing the rest of the scene.
TECHNIQUES: canvas 2D gradient sky, radial sun glow, quadratic-curve dune silhouettes, per-ridge slope shading, offscreen-canvas band slicing for sine heat-shimmer displacement, twinkling star field, DPR-aware resize
INTERACTION: SUN slider (0-100) lowers the sun toward the horizon; sky lerps to #1a0f2e, stars fade in, ridge highlights dim. Hover-free ambient scene; pointer only used on the slider. Caption "DUNE / DUSK" in tracked small caps.
