TITLE: Kaleido Ink
PROMPT: KALEIDO INK - kaleidoscope drawing tool, full-bleed. 8-fold mirror symmetry: strokes drawn to an offscreen canvas are stamped into 8 rotated copies around center every frame. Soft round ink brush, width varies with pointer speed; 4 ink swatch chips (crimson #c22e3a, gold #e0b13f, indigo #33408f, jade #2f8f6f). PAPER toggle swaps bg #f3ead8 <-> #101418. SPIN button slowly rotates the whole mandala; CLEAR wipes with a radial ink-blot animation. Everything auto-symmetric - drawing feels like spinning a kaleidoscope toy. Persistent canvas, no fade.
DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed drawing toy that turns the whole viewport into a kaleidoscope window. Every stroke lands on a persistent offscreen ink canvas that is composited each frame as eight rotated, alternating-mirror copies around the screen center, so a single gesture immediately blooms into a mandala. The brush is a soft round ink with a wide low-alpha halo under a dense core, and its width breathes with pointer speed — fast flicks lay thin lines, slow drags pool thick. A paper-warm palette (crimson, gold, indigo, jade) sits against warm paper or near-black dark paper, and a subtle radial vignette plus a small center pivot mark give the instrument a physical, hand-made feel.
TECHNIQUES: offscreen-canvas stamp compositing, 8-fold rotational mirror symmetry, speed-modulated round brush with dual-alpha ink layering, DPR-aware canvas resize with ink preservation, radial ink-blot clear animation with organic wobble, eased rotation spin, warm-paper vignette, frosted HUD pill deck with tracked-caps caption
INTERACTION: draw anywhere with pointer (touch or mouse) to lay symmetric ink; 4 swatch chips pick the ink color; PAPER toggle swaps light/dark paper; SPIN toggles slow rotation of the whole mandala; CLEAR wipes via a radial ink-blot animation.
