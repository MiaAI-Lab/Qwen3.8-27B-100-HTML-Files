TITLE: Tideglass — Sunlit Water

PROMPT: TIDEGLASS - sunlit water surface sim. Sky gradient #ffd9a0->#7fc8ff top third; water #0b3550->#061e2c. Two-buffer ripple simulation on canvas at 1/8 resolution: click/drag drops ripples; waves refract a sun disc + shimmer highlight field beneath. Specular fleck particles drift with wave height. HUD top-right: 'TIDE / GLASS' caps + thin wave-energy bar. On load: three staggered auto-ripples. DPR-aware; fit() called once at boot. Slow, luxurious, meditative. Water should look liquid, not tiled.

DESCRIPTION: A full-viewport canvas of slow, sunlit water. The upper third is a warm sky gradient (#ffd9a0 down to #7fc8ff) holding a soft sun disc with a bloom halo; below it the water deepens from #0b3550 to #061e2c. A classic two-buffer height-field ripple simulation runs at 1/8 resolution and is upscaled with smoothing so the surface reads as liquid, never tiled. Each frame, the sim's slope field refracts the sky+sun and the water's specular shimmer column, producing a real sun path across the water that bends and breaks under ripples. Drifting specular flecks shimmer with local wave height, and a quiet HUD in the top-right carries 'TIDE / GLASS' in tracked caps above a thin wave-energy bar that breathes with the simulation. Three staggered auto-ripples drop on load; the pace is slow, luxurious, meditative.

TECHNIQUES: two-buffer height-field wave equation at 1/8 resolution, ImageData pixel loop with gradient sampling for refraction, smooth upscale via drawImage + imageSmoothingQuality high, per-pixel sun disc + bloom + water shimmer columns, slope-based specular boost, drifting fleck particles coupled to wave height, wave-energy HUD bar, DPR-aware canvas with fit() at boot, pointer events with setPointerCapture

INTERACTION: click or drag anywhere on the water to drop ripples (drag leaves a continuous trail of small disturbances); the top-right HUD shows a live wave-energy bar that fills and decays as ripples travel; three auto-ripples fire on load at staggered times.
