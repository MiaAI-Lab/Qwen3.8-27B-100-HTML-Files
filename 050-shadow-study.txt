Caption 'STUDY I - THE KING, ONE LIGHT' serif italic.
DESCRIPTION: A single chess king drawn as a hand-authored silhouette path with three horizontal faceted tone bands, standing on a warm parchment stage. A default study palette (silhouette #17181a, facets #2a2c33, #3d4080, #6b7080) sits over a huge soft radial shadow cast toward the lower-left. A light-angle slider rotates the imaginary source: the facet bands slide up and down the form, the cast-shadow polygon re-aims and stretches (72 precomputed direction/length entries sampled in 5-degree steps), and the whole scene reads as one object lit by one lamp. Three material chips swap the palette with a two-layer crossfade. Clicking the king triggers a 360-degree spin: an eased Y-scale wobble narrows the silhouette to an edge-on sliver mid-turn while the shadow ellipse stretches long, then settles back.
TECHNIQUES: string-built layered SVG, hand-drawn king path with clip-path facet bands, precomputed 72-entry shadow polygon table, radial-gradient soft shadow, crossfade palette swap, eased 360 spin with Y-scale wobble
