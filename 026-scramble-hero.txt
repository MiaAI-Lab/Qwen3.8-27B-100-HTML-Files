TITLE: Scramble Hero
PROMPT: SCRAMBLE HERO - pitch black #0a0a0a, one hero sentence at a time from 5 phrases (first: 'WE BUILD INVISIBLE MACHINES'), each word its own span. Continuous decode: letters not yet locked show random glyphs (0-9 a-f section-sign) re-rolling every 90ms; a left-to-right sweep locks letters in; after full resolve hold 2s, next phrase. One word per phrase in accent #ff4d2e. Pointer hover over a word pins it (its letters stop scrambling). Bottom: 'SCRAMBLE / RESOLVE - v0.9' tiny mono + a tick bar showing phrase progress. No images, all type.
DESCRIPTION: A pure-typography decode ritual on pitch black. One hero sentence at a time cycles through five phrases, each word in its own span, every letter in a fixed-width slot. Unlocked letters churn through random glyphs from 0-9, a-f and the section sign, re-rolling every 90 milliseconds, while a left-to-right sweep locks real letters into place with a soft ink glow; the two or three letters just ahead of the sweep head burn in the accent orange-red. After full resolve the line holds for two seconds, fades out with a blur, and the next phrase begins - looping forever. One word per phrase carries the #ff4d2e accent with a warm text-shadow. A framed console wraps the hero: corner ticks, a RESOLVE SEQUENCE 01/05 eyebrow, a live SIGNAL status line (SCRAMBLING / RESOLVED / SIGNAL LOST) with a three-digit locked/total counter and blinking cursor, and at the bottom the tiny monogram 'SCRAMBLE / RESOLVE - v0.9' beside a five-segment tick bar that fills with accent color as each phrase's decode progresses.
TECHNIQUES: per-letter glyph scrambling with 90ms re-roll timer, left-to-right lock sweep with accent sweep head, word-span layout with fixed-width letter slots, accent glow via text-shadow, blur-fade phrase transitions, live status HUD with blinking cursor, five-segment progress tick bar, hover-pinned words, monospace console framing, scanline texture
INTERACTION: Pointer hover over any word pins it - its letters stop scrambling while hovered (pinned words get a pulsing accent underline); phrases auto-cycle with no buttons; the tick bar and SIGNAL status track decode progress live
