TITLE: Leaves / 140 - Wind 0.4
PROMPT: LEAF STORM - autumn wind. 140 leaves (canvas: small bezier leaf shapes, 6 colors #d05a2e, #e08a2e, #c23a2e, #a85a2e, #e0b13f, #8a3d33) falling through a curl-noise wind field with horizontal bias from the WIND slider (0-1), each spinning + flapping (rotation oscillation); two depth layers (far small/alpha .6, near large). Pointer = gust source: leaves within 200px get pushed away and spin faster. Ground: accumulating pile (height map, max 80px) - leaves settle and slowly fade into it. BG pale #f2e8d5 sky with a low #ffd27a sun. Caption 'LEAVES / 140 - WIND 0.4'.
DESCRIPTION: A full-viewport autumn wind scene rendered on a DPR-aware canvas: 140 leaves drawn as small bezier-curved shapes with a center vein, in six warm fall colors, drifting down through an analytic curl-noise wind field built from layered sinusoids so the flow is smooth and divergence-free in feel. The WIND slider (0-1) bends the field from a calm drift into a strong rightward gusting breeze with faster time evolution. Each leaf spins at its own rate and flaps with a sinusoidal vertical scale oscillation. Two depth layers - far leaves are smaller and drawn at 0.6 alpha behind the pile, near leaves larger and fully opaque in front. The pointer acts as a gust source: leaves within 200px are pushed radially away and spin faster, with a small "GUST" tag lighting up while a gust is active. At the bottom a height-map pile (96 bins, capped at 80px) accumulates where leaves land; settled leaves sink and fade into the pile before recycling back up, and the pile slowly decays so the ground breathes. The background is a pale cream sky gradient with a low amber sun and wide radial halo.
TECHNIQUES: analytic curl-noise wind field (layered sinusoids), bezier leaf shapes with vein detail, two-layer depth compositing (alpha .6 far / 1 near), per-leaf spin + flap oscillation, pointer radial gust force with 200px falloff, bin-based accumulating pile height map capped at 80px with neighbour smoothing and decay, low-sun radial halo, cream sky gradient, leaf settle/fade/recycle lifecycle
INTERACTION: WIND slider (0-1) controls horizontal bias and field speed; pointer on canvas acts as a gust source pushing leaves away within 200px and spinning them faster (GUST tag lights up); leaves continuously accumulate into a fading ground pile; caption shows live wind value
