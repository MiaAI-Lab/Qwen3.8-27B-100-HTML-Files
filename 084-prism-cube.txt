TITLE: Glass Cube
PROMPT: GLASS CUBE - a floating CSS-3D glass cube (6 faces rgba(200,230,250,.12), 1px #cfe8f5 edges, backdrop blur 6px so it refracts the background) rotating continuously (rotateX/rotateY), drag to spin with inertia. It floats on a slow sine bob; a soft floor shadow ellipse squashes inversely. Behind it: a horizontal light bar (white gradient strip) crosses the cube zone - draw three faint offset copies of the strip clipped to the cube region so 'refraction' reads. SPECTRUM toggle: the strip becomes a rainbow and faces tint rose/cyan/violet by orientation. Caption 'GLASS / CUBE - 6 FACES, LIVE'.
DESCRIPTION: A single floating CSS-3D glass cube built from six translucent faces (rgba(200,230,250,.12) fills, 1px #cfe8f5 edges, 6px backdrop blur) inside a perspective stage over a deep blue-violet night gradient with a subtle star grain. The cube spins continuously on rotateX/rotateY; pointer drag takes over with direct 0.45 deg/pixel control, and releasing hands the last velocity back to the physics loop as decaying inertia that eases toward the idle spin rate. A slow sine bob lifts and settles the whole assembly with a gentle counter-tilt, while a soft radial floor-shadow ellipse squashes and dims inversely to the bob height. Behind the cube, a clipped 'strip zone' holds three offset copies of a horizontal white light bar that drift and skew against each other frame to frame, so light visibly slides through the glass and the refraction reads even without a background. The SPECTRUM toggle recolors the strip as a rainbow, tints the six faces rose/cyan/violet, and modulates each face's inner glow by how much it points toward the camera (cos/sin of the live rotation), with a brief saturation pulse on toggle.
TECHNIQUES: CSS 3D transforms with perspective, backdrop-filter refraction, inertia-based drag physics, sine bob with inverse shadow squash, clipped multi-strip parallax light refraction, orientation-derived face tinting, radial gradient ambient lighting, pointer capture
INTERACTION: drag anywhere on the cube to spin it with inertia (release hands velocity back to the idle spin); SPECTRUM toggle button switches the light strip to rainbow, tints faces rose/cyan/violet by orientation, and pulses the stage saturation.
