TITLE: Today Weather — Minimal
PROMPT: TODAY WEATHER - minimal single-card weather app. Center: huge '23' degree readout (14vw, thin weight, #1c1d22 on #f4f2ec), condition word in tracked caps, and a CSS-only weather icon that morphs between states: SUN (disc + 8 slowly rotating rays), CLOUD (three overlapping drifting circles), RAIN (cloud + 4 falling drop loops), SNOW (cloud + 6 drifting flakes), STORM (cloud + flickering bolt). 5 chips switch state - each swaps icon + subtly tints the palette (rain cool #2f5fd8, sun warm). DESCRIPTION: A single centered card on warm paper (#f4f2ec) holds a huge thin-weight '23' degree readout, a tracked-caps condition word, and a fully CSS-built weather icon that cross-fades between five states: a sun disc with eight slowly rotating rays, a cloud of three drifting circles, rain with four falling drop loops, snow with six drifting flakes, and a storm with a flickering bolt. Five chips below switch the state, each subtly tinting the card, accent color and page background (cool blue for rain, warm gold for sun). A row of four tiny stat tiles (H/L, WIND, HUM, UV) updates with the state, and the big degree number eases to its new value. Hovering the icon makes it pulse.
TECHNIQUES: CSS-only weather icon (rotating rays, drifting circles, drop/flake loops, flickering clip-path bolt), cross-fade state morphing, per-state palette tinting, cubic-lerp number animation, tabular-nums stat tiles, paper-minimal layout
INTERACTION: 5 state chips (Sun/Cloud/Rain/Snow/Storm) swap icon and palette; hover the icon to pulse; stat tiles and degree readout animate on state change
