TITLE: Synth Extrude
PROMPT: SYNTH EXTRUDE - 3D extruded neon type. The word 'SYNTH' (or typed 3-8 char word) built from ~40 stacked text layers, each 1px translated, colored front #ff2e88 -> back #3d1a6e; the front layer carries a sliding linear-gradient chrome shine (background-clip: text). Drag rotates the whole word (rotateY +/-25deg, rotateX +/-12deg); hover a letter: all its layers spring 8px forward and shift hue. Floor reflection: mirrored layers, alpha .12, blurred. BG #0d0a18 with faint grid. Caption 'TYPE / EXTRUDE - 40 PLATES'. Chunky, glossy, 80s.
DESCRIPTION: An 80s arcade-plaquee of extruded neon type: each letter is a stack of 40 glyph layers translated back along Z, ramped from hot pink #ff2e88 on the face to deep violet #3d1a6e at the tail, with the front plate carrying an animated sliding chrome shine via background-clip text. The whole word floats on a dark #0d0a18 field with a faint two-tone grid and a soft center glow, swaying on an idle pendulum. A mirrored, blurred, low-alpha floor reflection mirrors the full extruded stack beneath it. A monospace input in the HUD lets you retype the word (3-8 characters) and the extrusion rebuilds.
TECHNIQUES: CSS 3D transform-style preserve-3d glyph stacking, per-layer translateZ depth ramp, linear-gradient color interpolation, background-clip:text animated chrome shine, drag rotation with momentum and idle sway, springy letter hover via cubic-bezier overshoot, flipped mask-faded floor reflection, faint grid background
INTERACTION: drag anywhere on the stage to rotate the word (clamped rotateY +/-25deg, rotateX +/-12deg) with momentum; hover a letter to spring its plates forward and shift hue; type a new 3-8 character word in the HUD input to rebuild the extrusion
