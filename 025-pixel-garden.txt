TITLE: Pixel Garden
PROMPT: PIXEL GARDEN - 16x10 big-tile farm (~6vmin tiles). Seasons auto-cycle every 40s: SPRING #7cc46a, SUMMER #e7d05a, AUTUMN #d0803f, WINTER #dfe8f0 with smooth tile tint lerp. Click: plant a seed -> 4 growth stages over 20s -> bloom sparkles; WATER tool doubles growth speed; HARVEST adds to score; in winter unharvested flowers frost over (desaturate + snow cap). Top bar: 'GARDEN 16x10 - SPRING' in tracked mono caps + harvest score. Tool chips bottom: SEED / WATER / HARVEST. Cozy, tactile, satisfying growth.
DESCRIPTION: A cozy big-tile farm rendered on a single canvas: a 16x10 grid of rounded tiles (~6vmin each) whose tint smoothly lerps through SPRING, SUMMER, AUTUMN and WINTER every 40 seconds, with a live season dot in the header and falling snow plus frost specks in winter. Clicking with the SEED tool plants a sprout that advances through four growth stages over 20 seconds - soil bump, two leaf pairs, bud, full five-petal bloom with a warm center and twinkling sparkle glints. The WATER tool doubles growth speed for 0.9s with a blue ring pulse and droplet burst; HARVEST pops a bloomed flower into a particle burst and increments the zero-padded harvest score in the tracked-caps top bar. Unharvested plants frost over in winter, desaturating toward pale ice and gaining a snow cap. Hover outlines the targeted tile; the whole piece is warm, tactile and monospaced like a little greenhouse HUD.
TECHNIQUES: DPR-aware canvas grid, per-tile seeded color variance, eased seasonal palette lerp, multi-stage plant drawing with quadratic stems and rotated ellipse leaves, particle bursts, drifting snowfall, frost desaturation with snow caps, hover targeting, tracked-caps monospace HUD
INTERACTION: Pointer: click/tap a tile to use the active tool - SEED plants, WATER doubles growth speed with a pulse, HARVEST scores and clears bloomed flowers; hover highlights the targeted tile; bottom chips SEED / WATER / HARVEST switch tools (keys 1/2/3 also); seasons auto-cycle every 40s
