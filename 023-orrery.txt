TITLE: Orrery - Interactive Solar System
PROMPT: ORRERY - interactive solar system. Deep space #05070d, faint starfield; glowing sun center; 8 planets on thin elliptical rings #2a3550, realistic colors (mars #c1553b, jupiter #d8b48a...), sizes stylized not to scale; relative periods compressed (Mercury fastest ... Saturn still visibly moving). Time chips: 1d/1w/1mo/1y per second + pause; click a planet: ring highlights and a glass info card shows name, AU, period, day length. Drag tilts the plane (rotateX 0-60deg), scroll zooms. Smooth eased motion.
DESCRIPTION: A cinematic canvas orrery on a deep-space ground with a slow two-tone nebula wash and a twinkling DPR-aware starfield. A glowing radial-gradient sun anchors the center while eight planets ride thin elliptical orbit rings in their real hues (Mars #c1553b, Jupiter #d8b48a, Saturn with a doubled translucent ring). Orbital periods are power-compressed so Mercury whips around fast yet Saturn still visibly moves; planet sizes are stylized, and a depth factor subtly grows/shifts bodies near the viewer. Clicking a planet lifts its ring into a glowing highlight and slides in a blurred glass info card with name, distance in AU, year and day length; the HUD captions the page in tracked caps with a monospace index line.
TECHNIQUES: canvas 2D starfield with sinusoidal twinkle, radial-gradient sun and planets, depth-sorted elliptical orbits, golden-angle phase seeding, power-law period compression, DPR-aware resize with boot-time fit, eased tilt/zoom interpolation, glassmorphism blur info card
INTERACTION: time chips (1D/1W/1MO/1Y days-per-second + PAUSE/RESUME toggle) on the top-right; click a planet to select it (orbit ring highlights, glass card shows name, AU, period, day length) or click empty space to deselect; vertical pointer drag tilts the orbital plane between 0 and 60 degrees; scroll wheel zooms the system smoothly
