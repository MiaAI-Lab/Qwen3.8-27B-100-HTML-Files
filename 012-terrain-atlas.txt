TITLE: Terrain Atlas

PROMPT: TERRAIN ATLAS - generative topographic map, full-bleed canvas, dark #0d1a12. Terrain height = value-noise FBM (5 octaves), contours via marching squares as thin #9fe6b8 lines at 12 elevation levels; 4 major elevation bands get translucent green (low)/ochre (high) fills. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom (0.4-3x); noise tiles via integer-lattice wrap so panning never runs out of terrain. HUD: fixed compass rose N, fake lat/lon readout derived from offset, scale bar. On load: slow auto-pan drift until first input. Integer edge-keys for contour loop walking - test it.

DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed generative topographic survey rendered on a single canvas over a deep forest-green ground. Height is computed from 5-octave value-noise FBM whose lattice wraps on an integer period, so the terrain is endless in every direction. Contours are extracted with marching squares at 12 evenly spaced elevation levels and stroked as thin sage-green lines (the four outermost bands slightly brighter), while the four major elevation bands are filled with translucent green-to-ochre washes. A minimal instrument HUD frames the map: sheet title and tracked-caps coordinates in the upper left, a conic-ring compass rose with red-tipped needle in the upper right, a drag/zoom hint plus band legend in the lower left, and a live scale bar with km readout and zoom multiplier in the lower right. A soft vignette focuses the eye on the map center.

TECHNIQUES: value-noise FBM with 5 octaves on an integer-wrapping periodic lattice, marching-squares contour extraction keyed by integer edge cases, one Path2D batched stroke per elevation level, translucent band fills, DPR-aware canvas resize, fake lat/lon and scale-bar derivation from pan offset, auto-pan drift until first input

INTERACTION: pointer drag pans the map (auto-pan drift stops on first input), mouse wheel or trackpad zooms from 0.4x to 3x anchored at the cursor position, the compass needle stays fixed to north while the world moves beneath it, and the coordinate readout and scale bar update live with every pan and zoom.
