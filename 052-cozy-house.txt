TITLE: The House at Maple 12
PROMPT: COZY HOUSE - isometric 2.5D house at night (canvas): warm windows #ffd27a (one flickers), chimney smoke particles, snow falling (150 flakes, 3 parallax layers), road with mailbox + streetlamp cone + tree. TIME slider DUSK->MIDNIGHT: sky #2a3560->#0d1226, snow rate rises, one window goes dark (someone sleeps). Click a window: toggle its light; click the chimney: smoke puff; click the tree: stars twinkle inside its canopy. Every ~30s a tiny cat silhouette crosses a window. Caption 'THE HOUSE AT MAPLE 12' soft caps. Quiet, warm, alive.
DESCRIPTION: A quiet isometric 2.5D cottage rendered on a DPR-aware canvas, sitting on a snow-dusted lane at the edge of dusk. The front and side walls carry four warm windows (#ffd27a) with soft glow shadows, one of which flickers like a candle behind glass; the roof line is capped with snow, a chimney breathes puffs of drifting smoke, and a road with a mailbox, a cone-lit streetlamp, and a snow-crowned evergreen complete the scene. A 150-flake snowfall runs in three parallax layers that accelerate as time deepens. The whole mood is governed by a single TIME slider sweeping dusk to midnight: the sky gradient darkens from #2a3560 toward #0d1226, stars and the moon gain presence, and past the 60% mark one window goes dark as someone falls asleep. Small living details keep it breathing: a tiny cat silhouette periodically walks across a lit window, and the caption 'THE HOUSE AT MAPLE 12' sits in soft tracked caps in the footer.
TECHNIQUES: canvas isometric 2.5D projection, DPR-aware resize with boot-time fit, layered gradient sky lerp between dusk and midnight palettes, three-layer parallax particle snowfall, smoke particle puffs with drift and growth, flickering window light via layered sine waves, shadow-blur glow for lit windows, pointer polygon hit-testing for windows/chimney/tree, periodic cat silhouette crossing, twinkling in-canopy star bursts, tracked-caps HUD and caption
INTERACTION: TIME slider sweeps dusk to midnight (darkens sky, raises snow rate, one window goes dark after 60%); click any window to toggle its light on/off; click the chimney for a triple smoke puff; click the tree for a burst of twinkling stars inside the canopy; hover highlights windows with a pointer cursor; every ~30 seconds a cat silhouette crosses a lit window
