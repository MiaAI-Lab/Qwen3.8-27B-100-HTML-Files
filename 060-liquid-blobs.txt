TITLE: Liquid Blobs
PROMPT: LIQUID BLOBS - metaball liquid field, deep #0d1017. Seven blobs, each 40-90px radius, 6 neon pastel colors, drifting on Lissajous paths with damped springs; one blob is 'attractive' - it springs toward the pointer. Render trick: draw each blob as ~60 overlapping low-alpha circles (soft accumulation) with globalCompositeOperation 'lighter' -> organic fused liquid look, then a subtle contrast pass. Slow hue drift per blob. VISCOSITY slider changes spring stiffness (jelly <-> syrup). Caption '7 BLOBS - LIQUID FIELD'. Fluid, glowing, hypnotic.
DESCRIPTION: A deep near-black field where seven glowing liquid masses drift in slow Lissajous orbits. Each blob is painted as a stack of ~60 overlapping low-alpha circles under additive ('lighter') compositing on a half-resolution offscreen buffer, so neighboring blobs accumulate into organic fused, metaball-like pools of neon pastel light - cyan, mint, periwinkle, lavender, coral, butter-gold and one hot pink. The pink blob is the attractor: it springs toward the pointer, so sweeping the mouse pulls a streak of light through the swarm. Every blob carries a faint white core highlight and a slow per-blob hue drift. A faint additive contrast pass and a center vignette deepen the fusion glow. The HUD reads '7 BLOBS - LIQUID FIELD' in tracked caps above a glassy control panel with the VISCOSITY slider.
TECHNIQUES: additive 'lighter' soft-circle accumulation (metaball-style rendering) at half resolution upscaled, damped spring physics chasing Lissajous path targets, pointer-attract spring for the 7th blob, per-blob slow hue drift, white core highlights, second faint additive pass as contrast lift, drifting ambient motes, soft pointer marker ring, radial vignette, DPR-aware offscreen buffer, speed clamping and edge containment
INTERACTION: move the pointer to pull the pink attractor blob through the field; VISCOSITY slider retunes spring stiffness from jelly (loose, bouncy) to syrup (heavy, slow), with a text readout
