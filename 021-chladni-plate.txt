DESCRIPTION: A near-black stage (#101114) holds a square canvas plate framed in brushed brass (#8a6a2f, 12px border). Thousands of 1px grains (#cfd6dd) begin scattered and, each frame, migrate against the standing-wave amplitude field A(x,y)=|cos(n x)cos(m y)| plus a little jitter until they settle into the classic Chladni nodal-line figure. Under the settled grain figure, faint gold lines (#d4af37, alpha 0.2) reveal the analytical nodal lines. A serif-italic readout shows the current pattern as 'PATTERN 5.7'. Grains are updated in chunks across frames so the tab never freezes.
TECHNIQUES: canvas grain relaxation toward zero-amplitude lines of a 2D standing-wave field, chunked per-frame updates to keep the loop responsive, brass-frame CSS border over a near-black stage, faint analytical nodal-line underlay, serif-italic pattern readout, DPR-aware resize
INTERACTION: Three range sliders drive the plate live - 'Mode n' (1-12), 'Mode m' (1-12), and 'Grains' (2000-10000, re-seed on change). Changing either mode morphs the figure as grains re-settle; changing grain count reseeds the population. The plate re-lays on window resize. No buttons - the sliders are the whole control surface.
