TITLE: Abyss Descent
PROMPT: ABYSS DESCENT - scroll-driven dive into the deep. Fixed-viewport scene; world is 4000vh and scrolling descends: light gradient #7fd4ff surface -> #020a14 hadal; HUD right: live depth meter mono (-0 m -> -4000 m), pressure bar, O2 that slowly depletes and resets after 10s idle. Depth bands: 0-200m plankton motes + fish silhouettes (parallax); 200-1000m glowing jellies (bezier bells, #9fd8ff); 1000-4000m anglerfish (lure dot + blink); 4000m+ hydrothermal vent (smoke particles + orange glow). Pointer adds gentle swim current. Caption 'ABYSS - DESCENT LOG'.
DESCRIPTION: A fixed full-viewport canvas over a 4000vh scroll track. The water column shifts from sunlit #7fd4ff at the surface through a blue-green twilight to near-black hadal, with god-ray shafts that fade as the viewer dives. Each depth band owns its fauna: drifting plankton motes and fish silhouettes with parallax near the surface, pulsing bezier-bell jellies with swaying tentacles in the twilight zone, a lone anglerfish with a blinking lure past 1000m, and hydrothermal chimneys with rising smoke puffs and orange glow at the bottom. A monospace telemetry HUD on the right shows live depth, pressure in atmospheres, and an O2 bar that depletes while scrolling and refills after ten seconds of stillness, plus a running descent log that stamps events as new zones are reached.
TECHNIQUES: layered canvas gradients, parallax bands, bezier jelly bells with radial glow, particle smoke system, procedural water column, monospace HUD telemetry, event logging
INTERACTION: scrolling drives the dive (4000vh world, fixed canvas); pointer movement adds a gentle swim current that displaces all creatures; HUD reacts live to depth and idle time
