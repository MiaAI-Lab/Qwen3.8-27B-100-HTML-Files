TITLE: Constellation Drift

PROMPT: CONSTELLATION DRIFT - deep #070b18 sky, 140 stars in 3 parallax depth layers (size by depth), gentle twinkle (alpha sine). Every ~90ms connect stars within 90px with thin lines (alpha by distance) so constellations shift and reform; 4 bright 'named' stars carry small-caps labels (VEGA, RIGEL, DENEB, POLARIS). Pointer = gentle gravity well bending nearby lines + spawning slow-drifting new stars at cursor. Bottom: 'CONSTELLATION / DRIFT' + live link counter. One canvas; additive glow only on the big stars via shadowBlur.

DESCRIPTION: A slow night-sky field on a single deep #070b18 canvas. 140 stars live in three parallax depth layers — faint far specks, mid drifters, and large near ones — each sized by depth and breathing with individual sine twinkle. Every ~90ms the sky re-solves its constellations: stars closer than 90px are joined by hairline links whose alpha falls off with distance, so figures keep shifting and reforming; stale links fade out rather than popping. Four bright named stars — VEGA, RIGEL, DENEB, POLARIS — carry tracked small-caps labels and bloom additively via shadowBlur (glow reserved for the big stars only). The pointer is a gentle gravity well that pulls nearby stars into the web, bends the lines, and continuously spawns slow-drifting new stars at the cursor, capped so the field stays balanced. A bottom HUD carries 'CONSTELLATION / DRIFT' in spaced caps beside a live link counter.

TECHNIQUES: single-canvas starfield, three parallax depth layers with pointer-driven parallax offset, per-star sine twinkle, O(n^2) neighborhood relink every 90ms with distance-falloff alpha and eased link fade-in/out, pointer gravity well with decaying per-star impulse, pointer-spawned transient stars with a capped recency queue, additive 'lighter' composite glow via shadowBlur on big stars only, small-caps tracked labels, live DOM link counter, DPR-aware fit() at boot

INTERACTION: move the pointer across the sky to bend the constellation lines and continuously spawn slow-drifting new stars at the cursor (tap/click drops a star instantly); the bottom-right HUD shows a live count of active links; four named stars (VEGA, RIGEL, DENEB, POLARIS) glow additively with labels.
