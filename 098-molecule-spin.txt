DESCRIPTION: A pure CSS-3D molecular viewer on a near-black blueprint grid. Atoms are radial-gradient spheres sized by element (carbon gray, oxygen red, hydrogen white), bonds are thin divs re-anchored and rotated between projected atom centers every frame. Coordinates rotate about X and Y axes, are perspective-projected, and everything is depth-sorted by z each tick so nearer atoms occlude farther ones. The molecule auto-rotates with a speed slider, can be grabbed and spun with pointer drag that imparts inertia on release, and optional tracked label chips name every atom. Three presets swap the structure live: H2O, CH4, and ethanol C2H5OH with its correct 9-atom, 8-bond geometry.
TECHNIQUES: css-3d sphere projection with perspective, per-frame bond div stretching and rotation, depth-sorted z-index compositing, radial-gradient shading, pointer drag with velocity inertia, staggered auto-rotation, mono caption HUD on faint grid background
INTERACTION: preset chips switch molecule, SPIN slider sets auto-rotation speed, PAUSE freezes motion, LABELS toggles atom name chips, drag with pointer spins the molecule with inertia on release
