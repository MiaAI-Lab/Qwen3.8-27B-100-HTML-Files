TITLE: Light Shaft - 400 Motes
PROMPT: LIGHT SHAFT - minimal cathedral interior. Dark #14100c walls, two tall arched window outlines #0d0a08; one strong diagonal light shaft (warm #ffd9a0, alpha .12 outer / .2 core parallelogram) from the upper-right window into the room. 400 dust motes (1-2px dots #ffe9c8): brightness = inside-shaft test (point in parallelogram) x twinkle; they drift slowly upward-Brownian. Pointer inside the shaft gently repels motes. SUN slider: shaft angle 30-60deg + brightness; motes outside are near-invisible (alpha .05). Occasionally one big mote catches the light (3x brightness for 1s). Caption 'SHAFT / 400 MOTES - 3:12 PM'.
DESCRIPTION: A hushed, minimal cathedral nave rendered on a full-viewport canvas: near-black warm walls (#14100c) with two tall arched window outlines in #0d0a08, the left one dark and dead, the right one the source of a strong diagonal warm shaft (#ffd9a0) that falls from upper-right into the room as a parallelogram with an alpha .12 outer band and a brighter alpha .2 core, plus a soft spill glow where it meets the window. 400 dust motes (1-2px #ffe9c8 dots) drift slowly upward with Brownian jitter; a per-frame point-in-parallelogram test decides whether a mote is inside the shaft, so in-shaft motes twinkle between their full brightness and dim while out-of-shaft motes sit at a near-invisible 0.05 alpha. Moving the pointer inside the shaft gently repels nearby motes with a soft radial force. A SUN slider (30-60 degrees) rotates the shaft and scales its brightness and the mote illumination in real time, with the angle shown in a tracked-caps mono HUD. Every few seconds a large in-shaft mote 'catches the light' - 3x brightness plus a pale halo for one second. The caption reads 'SHAFT / 400 MOTES - 3:12 PM' with a 'NAVE - NORTH LIGHT' sub-line.
TECHNIQUES: full-bleed DPR-aware canvas, arched window paths with mullions, parallelogram shaft from point-in-parallelogram basis vectors, dual-band shaft alpha (0.12 outer / 0.2 core) scaled by sun brightness, 400-mote particle system with upward-Brownian drift and per-mote twinkle phase, pointer repel force field gated by in-shaft test, sun-angle slider remapping shaft geometry and brightness, timed 'catch' spotlight with halo, seeded in-shaft half for instant readability, guarded rAF loop, tracked-caps mono HUD with clamp() fluid type
INTERACTION: SUN slider sets shaft angle 30-60 degrees (live angle readout) and scales shaft/mote brightness; pointer move inside the light shaft gently repels dust motes (radial soft force, motes outside the shaft are unaffected); no buttons, no keyboard; everything else is autonomous (drift, twinkle, periodic catch, recycle).
