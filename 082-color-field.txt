TITLE: Field Study 04 - After R.
PROMPT: COLOR FIELD - Rothko-like breathing rectangles. Three stacked horizontal fields (classic Rothko ratios ~35%/35%/30%), each rendered per-frame on canvas from very-low-frequency noise (64x40, upscaled with smoothing) so the edges wobble subtly and the color shimmers. Every 12s the palette crossfades (2s) through 6 schemes: maroon/olive/gold, red/blue/cream, violet/yellow/brown, teal/rust/white, black/red/amber, green/blue/buff. Pointer distance from center sets the 'restlessness' (breathing speed). Caption tiny: 'FIELD STUDY 04 - AFTER R.' Nothing else on the page.
DESCRIPTION: A full-viewport canvas painting in the spirit of late Rothko: three horizontal color fields at roughly 35/35/30 ratios, their seams rendered as softly wavy quadratic curves driven by a 16x11 low-frequency value-noise lattice that eases toward fresh random targets each frame. The whole piece breathes - edges wobble, fields shimmer through a 64x40 noise pass drawn with high-quality upscaling in overlay and soft-light blends, a mid-tone seam band feathers each boundary, and a radial vignette deepens the frame. Every 12 seconds the palette crossfades over 2 seconds through six hand-picked schemes (maroon/olive/gold, red/blue/cream, violet/yellow/brown, teal/rust/white, black/red/amber, green/blue/buff). Pointer distance from screen center sets 'restlessness', scaling noise evolution speed, edge amplitude and shimmer alpha. The only text is a tiny tracked-caps caption at the bottom.
TECHNIQUES: canvas per-frame paint, value noise lattice with eased targets, quadratic wavy boundaries, palette crossfade interpolation, overlay/soft-light noise shimmer, radial vignette, pointer-driven modulation, clamped easing, DPR-aware resize
INTERACTION: pointer/touch position: distance from center continuously sets breathing speed (restlessness) - edges wobble harder, shimmer intensifies. No controls; the caption is the only fixed element.
