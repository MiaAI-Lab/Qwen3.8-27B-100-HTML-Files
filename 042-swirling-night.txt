A big moon glows as a radial disc wrapped in rotating spiral halo arcs, a dark cypress flame rises at bottom-left, and six warm village lights flicker with halos over a swelled ground. A tracked-caps vertical caption 'NIGHT, SWIRLING' runs down the right edge.
