TITLE: Rain on Glass - City Bokeh Condensation
PROMPT: RAIN ON GLASS - rain condensation scene. Behind: city bokeh - 8 blurred light discs (warm #ffb454, cool #7fd4ff, white) drifting slowly (offscreen canvas). Glass: droplets spawn, grow by surface tension, then slide down leaving fading trails; each active drop distorts the bokeh (render bokeh through a 1/4-res distortion pass where drops offset samples - use willReadFrequently). WIND slider (0-1) tilts slides. SOUND toggle: filtered noise rain via WebAudio, created on first gesture. Moody, quiet, cinematic. Drips must look organic, never uniform.
DESCRIPTION: A quiet cinematic scene of rain condensing on a window above a night city. Eight additive blurred bokeh discs in warm amber, cool blue and white drift slowly on a quarter-resolution offscreen canvas behind the glass. Water droplets spawn organically - sometimes in small irregular clusters - grow by a surface-tension radius, then break loose and slide downward with per-drop wind bias and wobble, shedding fading wet trails and leaving faint residue scars where drips end. Each active drop refracts the city light through a per-pixel quarter-res lens pass that offsets and brightens bokeh samples inside the drop footprint, upscaled with low-quality smoothing for a soft defocused look. A vignette, tracked-caps HUD caption and glassmorphic controls keep it moody and understated.
TECHNIQUES: quarter-resolution offscreen bokeh layer with willReadFrequently pixel readback, per-pixel water-lens distortion pass, additive radial-gradient light discs, asymmetric destination-out trail and residue fading, DPR-aware canvas resize with boot-time fit, WebAudio low-pass filtered noise with LFO, cinematic vignette
INTERACTION: WIND slider (0-1) biases sliding droplets sideways with a live readout; SOUND toggle button starts filtered noise rain on first gesture (WebAudio created on demand) and mutes on second tap; tapping the glass seeds a bigger droplet at the pointer; droplets otherwise spawn, grow and slide autonomously
