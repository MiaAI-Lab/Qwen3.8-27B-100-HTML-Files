TITLE: Concrete Zine — Issue 07

PROMPT: CONCRETE ZINE - brutalist zine cover 'ISSUE 07 - CONCRETE POETRY'. BG #101010, white #f2f0ec blocks, single accent red #ff3b30. Massive display type (system-ui 800) that overflows: 'LOUD' at ~20vw with hard offset shadow; asymmetric grid: rotated text strip, boxed manifesto paragraph, barcode column of black rects, a slightly-rotated 'FREE' sticker circle. Hover: red blocks invert to black-with-red-text. Three scroll 'pages', each a different layout, same brutal vocabulary. NO border-radius anywhere, NO gradients - flat only. Crosshair cursor. Everything sharp, loud, uneven on purpose.

DESCRIPTION: A three-page brutalist zine built like a risograph cover: near-black #101010 ground, bone-white #f2f0ec blocks and one screaming #ff3b30 accent. Page one is the cover — 'LOUD' set at ~20vw in system-ui 800 overflowing the viewport with a hard offset red shadow, a strip of header cells, a rotated vertical caption strip, a tilted manifesto box with a flat red drop block, a black-on-white barcode column, and a star-burst 'FREE' sticker. Page two inverts the field to white with a giant stroked '07', stacked 'CONCRETE / POETRY' display type, a tilted quote block, and a hard-edged band of squares. Page three is the index: numbered rows on flat rules, a 'END.' slab, and a red marquee strip crawling along the bottom. No border-radius, no gradients, everything flat and deliberately uneven; the cursor is a crosshair and any red block flips to black-on-red on hover.

TECHNIQUES: flat two-block palette, oversized 800-weight display type with hard offset text-shadow, stroked outline type, rotated elements and asymmetric scroll-snap pages, CSS clip-path star-burst sticker, flexbox barcode columns, -webkit-text-stroke, keyframe-free marquee drift via rAF, crosshair cursor, flat drop-shadow blocks

INTERACTION: three scroll-snap pages with a fixed tracked-caps HUD that shows the current page (PG 01/03 - PG 03/03); hover on any red block inverts it to black background with red text; hover on 'LOUD' pushes its offset shadow; the marquee strip runs continuously and inverts on hover.
