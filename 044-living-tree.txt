TITLE: Living Tree
PROMPT: LIVING TREE - recursive tree that grows from seed. Center canvas: trunk + branches, depth 9, spread 24deg, length x0.72, tapered strokes; growth animates over 8s (parent->child, slight wobble overshoot). Constant wind: per-segment sway (sine, amplitude by depth); STORM toggle adds gusty noise + 60 leaf particles blowing off. Season chips: SPRING (buds #f2a4c0), SUMMER (leaves #4d9e5f), AUTUMN (#e08a2e + falling leaves), WINTER (bare #8a8f98). Sky swaps day/night gradient + stars on a toggle. RE-GROW replays. Caption 'SPECIMEN 07 - OAK'.
DESCRIPTION: A specimen-card style page where a recursive oak (depth 9, 24-degree branch spread, 0.72 length ratio, tapered round-cap strokes) grows from the soil over eight seconds, parent branches finishing before their children, with a subtle sine wobble-overshoot easing on each segment's extension. Wind never stops: every segment sways on its own phase with amplitude scaled by depth, and a global gust value adds low-frequency surge. The STORM toggle cranks the wind, adds crackling high-frequency jitter, and sheds up to 60 wind-blown leaf particles from the canopy tips; AUTUMN also lets a sparse trickle of leaves drift down in calm. Season chips recolor the terminal foliage — spring buds, summer leaves, autumn amber, winter bare — while a NIGHT toggle crossfades the canvas sky between a soft day gradient and a deep blue night filled with 90 twinkling stars. RE-GROW rebuilds the random tree and replays the growth animation.
TECHNIQUES: pre-order recursive branch generation with per-branch birth times, wobble-overshoot growth easing, per-segment phase-based wind sway with depth-scaled amplitude, gust state machine with noisy targets, canvas ellipse leaf particles with gravity/drag/rotation and lifecycle caps, two-pass day/night gradient sky with alpha-twinkling stars, DPR-aware canvas resize with fit() on boot and on resize, try/catch-guarded rAF loop
INTERACTION: four season chips (spring/summer/autumn/winter) recolor foliage; STORM toggle adds gusty wind and sheds leaf particles; NIGHT toggle swaps the sky gradient and shows twinkling stars; RE-GROW button rebuilds a fresh random tree and replays the 8-second growth; canvas resizes with the window
