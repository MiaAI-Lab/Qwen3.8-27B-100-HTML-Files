DESCRIPTION: A full-bleed meditative koi pond on a vertical gradient from #0e2a2f to #071a1e with slow, breathing concentric ripple rings drifting outward from a soft off-center glow. Seven koi wander the water: each has a filled bezier capsule body, two flapping bezier tail lobes driven by a per-fish sine phase (plus a smaller middle finlet and trailing pectoral strokes), a dark rim light, and — on four of the seven — clipped elliptical arc bands making 2-tone koi in white #f2efe6, orange #e2572b and black #191b1c. Steering is a gentle force model: a wandering target re-picked every 6-14s, edge-avoidance margins, slow sinusoidal drift, and soft separation so the koi never overlap. Clicking feeds the pond: 8 golden flake particles fall and dissolve, a dashed feeding halo and a soft expanding ripple appear at the click point, koi within 120px gain a strong steer toward the feed, and an optional WebAudio sine plink (lazy-created on first click, guarded in try/catch) rings softly. Top-left carries vertical small-caps "KOI / ZEN / POND" branding, bottom-left a monospace tracked-caps HUD, and a fading "click to feed" hint. A radial vignette and delta-time loop with a per-frame guard keep the scene slow and calm.
