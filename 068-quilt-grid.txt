TITLE: Quilt 8x8 - 14 Motifs
PROMPT: GENERATIVE QUILT - 8x8 patchwork in a wood frame (#7a5a3a, 24px border). Each patch = one of 14 SVG motifs (flying geenes, half-square triangle, pinwheel, log cabin, circle-in-square, zigzag, diamond-in-diamond, plus block...) in 5 fabric colors (rust #c25a3a, cream #f2e8d5, indigo #2f3f8f, sage #7a9e8f, mustard #e0b13f), seeded-random but with an adjacent-contrast rule (no identical color pairs across seams). Hover a patch: it lifts (scale 1.06 + shadow) with a motif-name chip. RE-SEW button: new seed, patches flip in staggered (rotateX). STITCH toggle overlays dashed seam lines. Caption 'QUILT 8x8 - 14 MOTIFS'.
DESCRIPTION: A generative patchwork quilt rendered as an 8x8 CSS grid inside a warm wood-tone frame (24px border with inset highlight, dashed inner basting line, and drop shadow). Each of the 64 patches is a runtime-built inline SVG (via container innerHTML) drawn from 14 hand-coded motifs - flying geese, half-square triangle, pinwheel, log cabin, circle-in-square, zigzag, diamond-in-diamond, plus block, quarter-square, strip set, sash center, crossroads, heart block and star point - in a 5-color fabric palette of rust, cream, indigo, sage and mustard. A mulberry32 seeded RNG drives layout with an adjacent-contrast rule: no two patches sharing a seam may carry the same ground fabric, so the board always alternates across every seam. Hovering a patch lifts it (scale 1.06 with shadow) and floats a monospace chip naming the motif; RE-SEW rolls a fresh seed and flips all patches back in with a staggered rotateX cascade; STITCH toggles a dashed white seam overlay around every patch.
TECHNIQUES: seeded RNG (mulberry32), adjacent-contrast color rule, 14 inline SVG motif generators built as innerHTML strings, CSS grid 8x8 patchwork, wood-frame bevel with inset gradients, hover lift + motif chip, staggered rotateX flip-in animation, dashed stitch seam overlay, live seed readout
