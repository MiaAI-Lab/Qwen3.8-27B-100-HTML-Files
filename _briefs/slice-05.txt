MARBLE INK - scroll paper-marbling. Long scroll page (#f5f0e6 paper) with a fixed full-viewport canvas behind: curl-noise flow field drives thousands of thin ink strokes (3 inks: #1e2a4a, #c0392b, #e8b13f) that marble across the sheet; as the user scrolls, the field's angle rotates (scrollY -> rotation + shear) so each of the 4 text sections reveals differently-marbled patterns. Four translucent paper cards hold short poetic paragraphs. Right edge: scroll progress bar. Deterministic seed. Strokes drawn once into a persistent canvas - only new strokes added as scroll progresses.
