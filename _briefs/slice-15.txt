043-day-plan.html
A DAY, BLOCKED - minimal time-block planner. BG #f6f4ee, ink #20211f, accent #3a6cff. Vertical timeline 06:00->23:00: left rail with mono times + hour hairlines; 7 colored rounded blocks (WAKE, DEEP WORK, LUNCH, GYM, REVIEW, CALL, SLEEP) with soft shadow; the red 'now' line #d63a3a with a dot sits at the real clock position and creeps; past blocks dim to 60%. Click a block: it expands showing 3 sub-bullets + a DONE toggle (satisfying strikethrough + tiny spark). '+ NEW BLOCK' inserts an editable contenteditable 30-min block. Keys up/down move focus. Calm, useful, pretty.

044-living-tree.html
LIVING TREE - recursive tree that grows from seed. Center canvas: trunk + branches, depth 9, spread 24deg, length x0.72, tapered strokes; growth animates over 8s (parent->child, slight wobble overshoot). Constant wind: per-segment sway (sine, amplitude by depth); STORM toggle adds gusty noise + 60 leaf particles blowing off. Season chips: SPRING (buds #f2a4c0), SUMMER (leaves #4d9e5f), AUTUMN (#e08a2e + falling leaves), WINTER (bare #8a8f98). Sky swaps day/night gradient + stars on a toggle. RE-GROW replays. Caption 'SPECIMEN 07 - OAK'.

NEON SIGN FACTORY - dark shop wall #14100e with subtle brick texture (repeating gradients); a neon sign of user text (default 'OPEN') drawn on canvas: each character stroked in a huge system font, two passes - wide glow (#ff2e88, alpha .25, shadowBlur 24) then core (#ffd9ef, no blur); FLICKER mode dims one random letter every ~1.4s (classic broken neon). Controls: text input (max 12 chars), color chips (pink #ff2e88, cyan #27e0ff, green #46ff9e, amber #ffb454), STYLE chips (SCRIPT/MONO/BLOCK font stacks), flicker toggle, HUM sound (WebAudio 120Hz buzz + crackles, gesture-created). Caption 'CUSTOM NEON - EST. 1987'.
