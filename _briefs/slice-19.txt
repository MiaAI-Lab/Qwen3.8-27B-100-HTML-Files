AURORA RESPONSE - interactive aurora borealis. Polar night #060913, stars, snowy ground silhouette bottom 15%. Four aurora curtains on canvas: each sampled along a sine path (120 segments), vertical gradient #46ff9e/#27d3f2/#8a7dff fading with height, waving on a time-evolving curl field. Pointer = solar wind: curtains within 250px get extra displacement + brighten; pointer speed drives a KP readout top-right (rises fast, decays slow) - a quick flick triggers a Kp spike and the whole sky flares. Hold still: curtains relax. Caption 'AURORA / RESPONSE - KP 6.4' live. The sky should answer you.
