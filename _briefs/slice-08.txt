022-kaleido-ink.html
KALEIDO INK - kaleidoscope drawing tool, full-bleed. 8-fold mirror symmetry: strokes drawn to an offscreen canvas are stamped into 8 rotated copies around center every frame. Soft round ink brush, width varies with pointer speed; 4 ink swatch chips (crimson #c22e3a, gold #e0b13f, indigo #33408f, jade #2f8f6f). PAPER toggle swaps bg #f3ead8 <-> #101418. SPIN button slowly rotates the whole mandala; CLEAR wipes with a radial ink-blot animation. Everything auto-symmetric - drawing feels like spinning a kaleidoscope toy. Persistent canvas, no fade.

023-orrery.html
ORRERY - interactive solar system. Deep space #05070d, faint starfield; glowing sun center; 8 planets on thin elliptical rings #2a3550, realistic colors (mars #c1553b, jupiter #d8b48a...), sizes stylized not to scale; relative periods compressed (Mercury fastest ... Saturn still visibly moving). Time chips: 1d/1w/1mo/1y per second + pause; click a planet: ring highlights and a glass info card shows name, AU, period, day length. Drag tilts the plane (rotateX 0-60deg), scroll zooms. Smooth eased motion.

024-rain-glass.html
RAIN ON GLASS - rain condensation scene. Behind: city bokeh - 8 blurred light discs (warm #ffb454, cool #7fd4ff, white) drifting slowly (offscreen canvas). Glass: droplets spawn, grow by surface tension, then slide down leaving fading trails; each active drop distorts the bokeh (render bokeh through a 1/4-res distortion pass where drops offset samples - use willReadFrequently). WIND slider (0-1) tilts slides. SOUND toggle: filtered noise rain via WebAudio, created on first gesture. Moody, quiet, cinematic. Drips must look organic, never uniform.
