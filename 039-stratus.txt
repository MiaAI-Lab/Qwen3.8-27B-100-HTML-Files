TITLE: Stratus
PROMPT: STRATUS - layered cloud sky. 5 parallax cloud layers (each pre-rendered once to offscreen canvases: 200-400 soft blurred blobs), drifting at different speeds, wrapping at edges. WIND slider changes speed + slight directional skew. TIME slider 0-24h smoothly lerps the sky palette (dawn #ffd9a8, noon #cfe8f5, dusk #f2a46a, night #101a2e) plus a star layer + moon at night; clouds tint by ambient light (multiply overlay). At noon, tiny 'v' birds on the far layer flap slowly. Caption 'STRATUS - 5 LAYERS' + live wind kts readout. Dreamy, slow, atmospheric.
DESCRIPTION: A dreamy layered cloud sky built from five offscreen pre-rendered stratus sheets, each 200-400 soft radial-gradient blobs with per-layer parallax speed, drift skew and depth. The sky palette keyframes smoothly across the 24-hour cycle from night indigo through dawn peach, pale noon blue, dusk orange and back; a star field and crescent moon fade in at night. Clouds are re-tinted by the ambient light of the current hour so they glow white at noon and sink to blue-grey at night. Four tiny v-birds flap slowly across the far layer around noon. A glass panel at the bottom holds the two controls; the top HUD carries the caption and a live wind readout.
TECHNIQUES: offscreen pre-rendered cloud sheets (seeded radial-blob compositing), per-layer parallax with edge wrap, directional skew via canvas transform, keyframed 24h palette lerp, time-gated star and moon layers, ambient re-tinting on time change, v-bird flapping, blurred glass control panel, DPR-aware resize
INTERACTION: WIND slider (-50..50) sets drift speed, sign for direction, and slight skew of every layer, with a live "WIND n KTS" readout; TIME slider (0-24h) lerps the sky palette, star/moon visibility and cloud tinting, with a live HH:MM clock in the HUD. Caption reads 'STRATUS / 5 LAYERS'.
